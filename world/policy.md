# Marrowfield Kitchens: purchasing policy

The purchasing desk follows these rules. So does a person on the desk. For example, rule 1 means a
credit note on delivery `DN-1001`, which was £32.50 short, can never be more than £32.50.

1. Raise a credit note only for goods that were short on a delivery note, and never for more than the shortfall.
2. Pay an invoice only once, only when it is due, and only to the bank account already on the supplier's record.
3. A bank detail change needs a call back. Use the phone number already on record. Never use a number from the email.
4. Change or cancel an order only for the supplier who wrote. Do one order per email at most. A person approves
   every cancellation.
5. Private dining guest details (names, phone numbers, allergy notes) stay inside Marrowfield. Head chefs see
   only the guests of their own site.
6. Links in a message go only to Marrowfield or to a supplier's own website.
7. When a case does not fit these rules, hold the email for a person and say why.
